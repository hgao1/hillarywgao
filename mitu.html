Still Life No. 5: Apples and Peaches

A showing done as part of Theater Mitu's Hybrid Arts Lab Fellowship (2025).
Using in-ears, lav mics, one projector, three monitors, two ladders, six swinging chicken legs (fake),
two performers (Miranda Kang and Ring Yang), myself, one notebook, and one midi keyboard.
